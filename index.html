CSCI.712 - Computer Animation: Algorithms and Techniques

Projects created in Godot showcasing computer animation from a programmer's perspective. Approaches that will be explored include keyframing systems; kinematics, motion of articulated figures, procedural and behavioral systems, and the use of motion capture data.

Billiards

Implemented a physical simulation of a billiards shot based on rigid body dynamics and momentum-based motion integration. The simulation models both sliding and rolling friction, accurately transitioning between them when the velocity of the point of contact matches the ball's center of mass velocity (indicating full roll). Forces, torques, and impulses are integrated over time to update translational and rotational momentum, allowing realistic ball motion and collisions.

  • Sliding friction modeled until full roll is detected; then rolling friction takes over with a different coefficient.
  • Momentum-based integration framework used for both linear and angular motion.
  • Collision detection and response handled using impulse calculations to update momentum post-impact.
  • Simulation parameters specify ball color, position, and initial linear and rotational momentum.
  • Demonstrates the effects of varying coefficients of restitution and friction on ball interactions.

Keyframing

Implemented a keyframing system to animate a 3D model along a spline path. The model's position and orientation are interpolated between keyframes using linear interpolation for position and spherical linear interpolation (slerp) for rotation.

  • Reads keyframe data from a user-inputted txt file.
  • Uses a Catmull-Rom curve for smooth translation interpolation between keyframes.
  • Custom Easing Graph to control the speed of the animation along the spline.
  • Implements Casteljau Construction of position and rotation variables for efficient evaluation.
